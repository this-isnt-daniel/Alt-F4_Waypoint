# Waypoint Seed Scenarios

## Master Data Fidelity

**Note:** The current seed implements a subset of 24 outlets and 5 vehicles to demonstrate edge-cases deterministically. This is a demo subset, not the canonical 120-outlet, 60-vehicle competition master data.
The architecture supports canonical datasets. To use them, supply `outlets.csv` and `vehicles.csv` inside a `/app/data/` directory or mount it during runtime, and the backend seed script will automatically ingest them.

## 8. IMPORTANT TRUTH

The seed does not fake completed downstream actions. Confirmed orders remain confirmed until planning. Loaded trips remain loaded until Driver execution. POD and ReceiptConfirmation are generated by real workflow actions.
